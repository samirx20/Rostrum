# The one list of 2D blend modes: how a layer mixes with the layers under it. Read by the Python layer
# (rostrum2d/blends.py: the `blend` field's options, its inspector labels, the Lottie export) and by the engine
# at compile time (engine/render/src/scene/blends.rs: the field check and the CSS the painter reads).
# Columns, separated by " | ": name in the document | CSS mix-blend-mode the painter draws | Lottie `bm` | label.
# Every CSS value here reaches Skia (vendor/blitz-paint/src/blend.rs → anyrender_skia scene.rs `SkBlendMode`).
normal | normal | 0 | Normal
multiply | multiply | 1 | Multiply (darkens)
screen | screen | 2 | Screen (lightens)
add | plus-lighter | 16 | Add (light adds up)
overlay | overlay | 3 | Overlay (contrast)
soft-light | soft-light | 9 | Soft light
hard-light | hard-light | 8 | Hard light
darken | darken | 4 | Darker of the two
lighten | lighten | 5 | Lighter of the two
color-dodge | color-dodge | 6 | Colour dodge (burns bright)
color-burn | color-burn | 7 | Colour burn (burns dark)
difference | difference | 10 | Difference
exclusion | exclusion | 11 | Exclusion (softer difference)
hue | hue | 12 | Hue only
saturation | saturation | 13 | Colour strength only
color | color | 14 | Colour only (keeps brightness)
luminosity | luminosity | 15 | Brightness only
