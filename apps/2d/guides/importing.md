# Importing pictures and files

For a person using the Rostrum2D app: bring a picture, a video, a PDF page, a Photoshop file or a Figma frame into your project.

## 1. Open the Import panel

1. Click **Import** at the top. The panel opens at the right of the canvas.
2. Click **Import** again, or **Close**, to put it away. Only one of Import, Export and the problems list is open at a time.

## 2. Choose where it comes from

1. **From** has two choices: **A file** and **Figma**.
2. With **A file**, click **Choose…** and pick the file. The name shows beside the button ("none chosen" until you do). You can pick pictures (PNG, JPG, WEBP, BMP, GIF, TIF), videos (MP4, MOV, WEBM, MKV, M4V, AVI), SVG, PDF and PSD.
3. The panel then shows only what that kind of file needs.

## 3. Settings for the file you chose

- **Page** (PDF only): which page to bring in.
- **Text in file** (an SVG that has text): **Editable text** makes real text layers you can change. **Outlines (looks the same)** keeps the shapes of the letters exactly, but you cannot edit the words.
- **Keep original size** (SVG, PDF, PSD, Figma): off, the page is fitted into your picture. On, it keeps its own size.

## 4. Bring it in

1. Click **Import**. A progress line shows while it works, and **Cancel** appears.
2. When it is done, the panel says what came across. It also lists anything that did **not** come across, under "Didn't come across". When everything came across it says "Everything came across."
3. The new layers appear in **Layers**. Pictures and layers from a PDF, PSD or SVG can be moved and changed like any other layer.

## 5. A single picture or video

You can also use **+ Add**, then **Image** or **Video**, and pick the file there. It is the same result for one picture or clip.

## 6. Figma

1. Choose **Figma** under **From**.
2. Fill in **File key** (from the file's address in your browser), **Frame** (the frame's name; the first one if you leave it empty) and **Token** (your own Figma access token; it is not saved).
3. Click **Import**.
