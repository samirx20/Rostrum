# Rostrum3D document (`.trd`)

*Team Hem, H12, 2026-10-21. Code: `apps/3d/python/rostrum3d/`. The fields of every type, their ranges, defaults, `valid_when` and importance: `rostrum3d/fields.py` (the one table).*

```
doc ─ items     ─ group  ─ children (items) ─ keys
                ─ import ─ children (items), parts ─ keys      (a USD file by reference; parts = overrides on prims inside it)
                ─ rig    ─ children (items) ─ keys      (a camera rig: orbit / crane / dolly, handheld)
                ─ mesh ─ bones (bone ─ bones … ─ keys) ─ keys      (a bone chain, each bone inside the one before)
                  mesh ─ paints ─ paint ─ strokes (stroke | fill) ; mesh ─ paintmasks ─ paintmask   (the painting kit)
                ─ clay ─ steps (claystep: pieces and strokes, in order) ─ keys ; clay ─ paints, paintmasks (as a mesh)
                ─ light | camera ─ keys
    ─ materials ─ material
    ─ shots     ─ shot
    ─ poses     ─ pose
```

- **Units:** metres, Y up, degrees, seconds. Colours are `#rrggbb` (sRGB); the compiler makes them linear.
- **Transform on every item:** `x y z rx ry rz sx sy sz visible`, relative to the parent; rotation is applied X, then Y, then Z (USD `rotateXYZ`).
- **Aim:** `aim` = `none` | `point` (`aimX aimY aimZ`) | `item` (`aimItem`). The compiler turns it into `rx ry rz` every frame so the item's −Z (where cameras and rect/disk lights face) points at the target, Y as close to up as it goes, no roll. While aimed, `rx ry rz` are not shown or accepted (rule of individuality).
- **Rigs (H21):** a `rig` holds the camera (or anything); `rig` = `orbit` (`radius height angle`, head faces the centre) | `crane` (`armLength lift swing`, level head) | `dolly` (`travel` forward along −Z); every rig has `pan tilt` and a seeded `handheld` drift (`shake shakeFrequency shakeMove seed`). Its own `x y z rx ry rz` are the base (no aim, no scale). The compiler (`rostrum3d/rigs.py`) turns it into a group with its transform baked per frame, Euler angles kept continuous; the core never sees a rig. How-to: `guides/camera-rigs.md`.
- **Mesh shapes:** `box sphere cylinder cone capsule plane disc torus` (cone and capsule: `radius height`), and three flat outlines the core extrudes `depth` deep (with `bevel`, `segments`, `smooth`; the faces can take their own materials: `frontMaterial bevelMaterial sideMaterial backMaterial`, `""` = the mesh's `material`, bevel `""` = front's): **`text`** (`text font weight size tracking lineHeight align`; font = Inter, Recursive, RobotoFlex or an absolute .ttf/.otf; `size` is the em in metres; `tracking` in thousandths of an em), **`svg`** (`svgFile` or `pathData`, `svgWidth`), **`path2d`** (`doc2d` = a 2D item id in the same project folder, `layer` = a path layer id in it, `svgWidth`). The outline is centred on the item origin (text: horizontally per `align`, vertically capital-height to last baseline; drawings: their ink's middle), so the item spins around its middle. `rostrum3d/outline*.py` make the outlines (HarfBuzz shaping, skia-pathops merge, flattened to ~0.1 mm or 0.2 % of the size); the compiler sends them as mesh shape `"outline"` and drops the document-only fields. Drawings: filled shapes only, `fill="none"` skipped, a white shape on top is another filled shape, not a hole.
- **Painting** (brief 20, `paint_fields.py`, guide `painting`): a mesh is a painting-kit canvas: `paints` (paint layers,
  bottom first, each with `channel`: colour, roughness, height, metal or glow, and its `strokes` in canvas pixels) and
  `paintmasks`; `paintSize` (the square picture's side, default 2048) and `uvs` (`mesh.unfold`: one u, v per face corner
  of the mesh as drawn, flat). The compiler gives a painted mesh its own material `<id>__paint` with the painted
  pictures (`paint_scene.py`). A clay is a canvas too (3d-cl4, `clay_paint.py`): `paintSize`, and its strokes may be kept
  on its surface instead of in pixels: `anchors` [[x, y, z, nx, ny, nz, pressure], …] in its own space and `reach` (brush
  radius, m); they are laid on the unfolded surface the core makes for each shape (node `paint`, a CLS1 file), so the
  paint stays on its spots through reshapes. Channel `height` is shown as Relief.
- **Clay (CL1, `clay_fields.py`, `ops_clay.py`, guide `sculpting`):** a `clay` is a lump to sculpt: transform, `material`, `detailMode` auto | fixed and `detail` (the smallest grain in metres, the core's voxel size, used when `detailMode` is fixed; not animatable) and the *next* stroke's brush settings (`brush` add | carve | smooth | pull | flatten | crease | mask, `brushSize` radius m, `brushStrength` 0..1, `brushFalloff` 0 soft .. 1 hard, `brushSpacing` 0.05..1 of the radius, `frontOnly`, `symmetry` off | x, `accumulate`; valid while sculpting, not animatable). **Detail auto** (`clay_detail.py`): for each shot whose camera sees the clay (a sphere round its pieces' `at` / `to` + radius, mirrored copies too, through the clay's world transform; strokes ignored), at its first, last and up to 7 frames between, one pixel at the clay's nearest point = distance × sensor width / (focal length × shot width); detail = 1.5 × the smallest, rounded to 0.1 mm, clamped 0.0002..0.05; no shot sees it → its radius / 100. A camera move changes it (and the cache) only when the rounded number changes. Its shape is its `steps`, applied in order, each a `claystep` in the clay's own space: `step` = `piece` (`shape` sphere | capsule, `at`, `to` (capsule), `radius`, `mode` add | carve, `blend`, `symmetry`) or `stroke` (`brush size strength symmetry accumulate falloff spacing frontOnly` copied from the clay when made, `invert` (Ctrl's opposite: flatten puffs, crease pinches, mask unmasks) as given, and one path: `dabs` = `"clay/<hash>.dabs"`, a file in the project; `dabList` = the same dabs inline; or `path` + `dir`, an agent's world stroke the core lands on the clay); optional `name`. **Dab files** (`clay_dabs.py`): little-endian `b"CLD2"`, uint32 n, n × 11 float32 (`a.xyz n.xyz b.xyz radiusScale strengthScale`, the scales pen pressure, 1 1 without a pen), or CL1's `b"CLD1"` with n × 9 (scales 1), still read; dabs that are all 9 numbers are written as CLD1 so CL1's files and keys stay; the name is the first 32 hex of the file's sha256, so files are never rewritten and undo never deletes them. The document's tidy (`KIND.tidy`) saves a call's inline `dabList` into a file and names it in `dabs` when the document has a project folder; without one (tests, scripts) the dabs stay inline. **Step ids:** operations count `<clay>.step_N`, scripts `<clay>.sN`, so a script re-run never takes a person's step id and keeps the person's steps (a piece the script adds later goes after the script's own pieces, so before steps a person added after them). **Compile** (`clay_scene.py`): props `detail` (the resolved number) `material smooth` + transform (no brush settings, no `detailMode`), `steps` with `key` = sha256 (first 24 hex) of the previous key + the step's canonical JSON (dabs by hash name, numbers as floats), the dabs as an absolute path or an inline `dabList`, and `cache` = the shared cache's `clay/<key>/clay.vdb` (key of the last step key, `detail` and `rostrum3d-clay@3`); a stroke step also sends `falloff spacing frontOnly invert`. The core compares keys to know what it already holds.
- **Mirrored copies (CL6, `mirror.py`, `ops_mirror.py`, guide `modelling`):** a mesh with `mirror` (the original mesh's id) and `mirrorOn` (a clay's id) is the original reflected through the clay's own X = 0 plane, per frame (`resolve.Frames`): every field and key is the original's, the transform is the reflected one (a shape the same from both sides is turned back so its scale stays positive; text and editable meshes keep a negative X scale). Its own stored fields are not used, except `name` and its `shape` (kept so shape-only edits sent to it are accepted). `mesh.set` on a copy writes the original (a move reflected back; refused when the original's place is worked out by aim, follow, stick, pivot or keys). `material.assign`, `material.split`, `material.from` (`to`), `paint.add` and `paintmask.add` aimed at a copy act on its original (kind.py wraps them once: `mirror.REDIRECTED`). Anything else that would set something on the copy alone (keys, an aim, follow, bones, points) fails the document check, naming the original. `mirror` and `mirrorOn` are group "Mirror data" (`fields.DATA_GROUPS`): never rows on the screen. `item.remove` of the original or the clay removes the copy. `mesh.mirror` (`on` = the clay) makes one; placing on a clay with `symmetry` x off its middle makes one in the same step. The compiler sends a copy as a plain mesh (no `mirror`, `mirrorOn`): nothing new for the engine.
- **Skies (CL6, `skies/`):** a dome's `texture` may name a sky that comes with the app, `"sky:<key>"` (`sky:studio`, Poly Haven's Studio Small 09, CC0); the compiler sends that file's absolute path, so a project opens on another computer. A new scene (`start_scene.py`) holds `ground`, the dome `sky` on `sky:studio`, `shot_1_camera` and the shot `shot_1` ("Shot 1").
- **Keys:** the 2D key model (`t` seconds, `v` or `c`, `ease`), ids `<item>.<field>.k<ms>`; evaluator, easing table and the four key operations are `rostrum_core.keys`. Animatable: every number and colour field of an item, except a mesh's shape sizes (the core builds a mesh once). Materials and shots don't animate in v1.
- **Timing (brief 17):** `timing` on every item that has a transform (not rigs, not domes) = `smooth` (default) | `on2` | `on3`. On n, every value is held for n frames: frame f shows the values of frame `start + ⌊(f − start) / n⌋ · n` (counted from the shot's first frame), aims and pivots included. An item's own `on2` / `on3` wins; `smooth` follows its nearest group's. The compiler does the holding (`compile._Frames.values`), so the viewport and the render (both `compile.describe`) step alike; the core never sees `timing`. Shown (`valid_when` fact `animated`) only once the item or something inside it has keys; a script may give it before its keys.
- **Bones (brief 19):** a mesh's `bones` list holds its chain, each `bone` `{x y z (head, in its parent's space: the mesh's own space for the first, else the bone before it), rx ry rz (Turn, the only keyable fields), length, bones, keys}`; a bone runs along its own +Y from its head, so the next bone's head is `(0, length, 0)`. The mesh's `skinned` (Bend with bones, shown when it has bones: fact `boned`) is never sent; the compiler sends the chain only as the mesh node's `skin` (ENGINE_CONTRACT.md § 2, `rostrum3d/skin.py`): rest heads and tails, and per frame each bone's move from rest. Made by `bone.chain` (points → bones, one step), `bone.bind` (off / on). `rostrum3d/ops_bones.py`.
- **Poses (brief 17):** doc-level `poses`, each `{id, type: "pose", name, fields: ["lid.rx", …], values: [−105, …]}` (field `<item id>.<field>`, one number each). Made by `pose.save` (x y z, rx ry rz unless aimed, sx sy sz of each target at a time), keyed back by `pose.apply` (at a time; `share` 0..1 = a breakdown between the neighbouring keys); `pose.rename`, `pose.remove`. Items deleted later are skipped on apply and named. `rostrum3d/ops_poses.py`.
- **Shots:** `camera` (a camera's id), `fps`, `start`, `end` **in seconds**, `width`, `height`, `background`. Frames run from `round(start × fps)` to `round(end × fps) − 1` (a 2 s shot at 24 fps = frames 0..47); frame *n* is at *n* / fps seconds.
- **Ids:** items and materials become USD prim names, so letters, digits and `_` only. Parts are `<import>.<path with _>`; keys come from their content.
- **References are checked** after every change: a mesh/import/part's `material`, a shot's `camera`, an aim's `aimItem` must name a real thing of that type. Deleting a material clears it from its users in the same step; deleting a camera a shot uses is refused until the shot names another.

## Public API (what the engine client and MCP server use)

- `rostrum3d.kind.KIND` (also `rostrum3d.KIND`), `REGISTRY`: the document kind and its operations.
- `rostrum3d.compile.describe(data, shot_id)` → the scene (ENGINE_CONTRACT.md § 2).
- `rostrum3d.compile.shot_info(data, shot_id)` → `{id, camera, fps, start, end, width, height}`; `start`/`end` are **frames** (inclusive).
- `rostrum3d.script.run_source(source, name)` → a tree for `doc.run_script(name, tree)`.
- `rostrum3d.engine.Engine.sculpt(**req)` (a live stroke: begin / dab / end / cancel) and `.clay_info(item)` (the clay as the core holds it: hash, steps, voxels, memoryMb, blocks).

Operations: `group.add mesh.add light.add camera.add rig.add import.add part.add material.add shot.add`, `clay.add clay.lump clay.shape clay.stroke claystep.remove`, `item.remove item.rename item.arrange item.aim`, `material.assign material.remove shot.remove`, `mesh.unfold`, the painting kit's `paint.add/set/move/remove/duplicate/clear/unlock stroke.add/set/remove fill.add fill.set paintmask.add/set` on meshes and clays (a clay stroke kept on its surface: `anchors`, `reach`), `key.add key.remove key.clear value.set`, and one `<type>.set` per type (`mesh.set`, `light.set`, `camera.set`, `material.set`, `shot.set`, `part.set`, `key.set` …).
