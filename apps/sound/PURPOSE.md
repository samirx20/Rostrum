# What RostrumSound is for

*Confirmed by Sam, 2026-10-23. When this and another doc disagree, this wins.*

RostrumSound makes the sound of a video: its **music**, its **voice** and the few **real sounds** the picture needs,
mixed and finished to the level of the reference films (Apple launch films, Fortiche, film-grade sound).

## What it does
1. **Music first.** Compose and arrange the score with real-quality instruments: keys, strings, choir, drums,
   bells, synths and pads. Movement in the picture is answered musically: a string rise, a cymbal swell, a piano
   run, a struck bell, a hit on the beat, a held breath of silence.
2. **Voice.** Record, clean and place voice-over and dialogue; speak lines from text; keep lips and words in time.
3. **Real sounds, only where the picture truly needs them.** A button click on a product shot, metal, glass, a door,
   a footstep: recorded sounds of real things, from the person's own library or a professional one, placed exactly.
4. **Finish.** Mix, master and deliver at the right loudness for where it's going, with stems for the Editor.

## What it doesn't do
- **No cheap effects.** No synthetic swishes, whooshes or "transition" sounds, and nothing shipped as a preset that
  wouldn't stand next to a professional library. Silence is better than a mediocre sound.
- No automatic sound on every movement. A move earns a sound only when a person or agent decides it needs one,
  and then it is music or a real sound.

## How we hold the bar
- A sound ships with Rostrum only if Sam has heard it and it holds up next to the reference set.
- Agents and guides score with instruments first; they never reach for an effect to fill space.
