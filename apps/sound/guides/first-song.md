# Your first song

For a person using the RostrumSound app. This page takes you from an empty song to something you can hear and keep.

## 1. Start a new song

1. Click **File** at the top, then **New song** (or press Ctrl+N). A new song is empty and called "Untitled song — not saved yet".
2. It is kept for you as you work, but it is thrown away if you leave without naming it (see step 7).

## 2. Add a track

1. Click **+ Track** at the top.
2. Pick what you want to start with: **Piano**, **Strings**, **Pad**, **Bass**, **Synth**, **Drums**, **Voice (spoken lines)** or **Audio (recordings, sound files)**.
3. The new track appears as a row at the bottom and is selected. The three pages at the top are **Instruments**, **Timeline** and **Mix**. The row bar at the bottom stays on every page.

## 3. Choose or change the instrument

1. Click **Instruments**. Each track has a card. A card shows only the controls that apply to its instrument.
2. The **Instrument** picker uses plain words: **None (clips only)**, **Built-in sound**, **Sampled (SFZ file)**, **Drum kit**, **Sampled drum kit**, **Played instrument**, **Synth**, **Spoken voice**, **Plug-in instrument**.
3. With **Built-in sound**, a second picker called **Sound** chooses which one. Choices are in words, such as **Grand piano**, **Warm pad** or **Sub bass**.
4. A track set to **None (clips only)** says "Plays recorded clips only. Pick an instrument to write notes here."
5. You can change the instrument at any time. The notes stay.

## 4. Write notes

1. Click **Timeline**, and click the track's name in the bottom rows so it is selected. Its notes show as bars on a keyboard.
2. **Double-click** empty space to add a note. Drag a note to move it in time and pitch (it snaps to a quarter beat; hold **Shift** to drag freely).
3. Click a note and press **Delete** to remove it.
4. For a **Drums** track, each drum has its own row; the same double-click and drag work there.
5. Zoom with **Ctrl + wheel**; scroll in time with **Shift + wheel**. **Ctrl 0** shows the whole song.
6. A mistake? Press **Ctrl+Z** to undo it (**Ctrl+Y** or **Ctrl+Shift+Z** to redo). Every drag is one undo step. The top bar counts your changes and says what happened last.

## 5. Play it

1. Press **Space**, or click the play button at the top, to hear the song from the playhead.
2. Click an empty spot in a track's row to move the playhead there.
3. **M** on a track's row mutes it, **S** plays it alone.
4. After an edit the sound is prepared again in a moment, so playback follows what you changed.

## 6. Set the tempo and key

Click the tempo (or key) near the top: the song box opens. It has **Tempo**, **Key** and **Section here**. Change them when you like; notes keep their beats.

## 7. Save it

1. Click **File**, then **Save…**. Type a name and choose where. By default songs go in `Documents\Rostrum Sound`.
2. The song becomes a folder of its own. From now on every change is saved as you make it, so there is no Save to press. (Ctrl+S just tells you so.)
3. Later: **File**, **Open…** and choose the song's folder, or pick it from the recent songs in the same menu.
4. **Save a copy as…** makes another folder to experiment with.

If you leave an unnamed song that has changes, the app asks **Keep this song?** with **Save…**, **Don't keep** and **Cancel**.
