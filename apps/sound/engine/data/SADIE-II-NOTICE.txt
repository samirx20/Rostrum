hrir_sadie2_d2.bin holds head-related impulse responses taken from the SADIE II database
(Spatial Audio for Domestic Interactive Entertainment), subject D2 (a KEMAR dummy head),
HRIR WAV release, at azimuths 0, ±30, ±90, ±110 and ±135 degrees, elevation 0, at 44.1, 48 and
96 kHz. Source: https://www.york.ac.uk/sadie-project/database.html (Zenodo record 12092466, D2.zip).

Changes made for Rostrum: the nine directions above were extracted, re-ordered to Rostrum's azimuth
convention (negative = left; SADIE's is counter-clockwise), converted to 32-bit float and scaled so the
front pair averages unit energy.

Reference: C. Armstrong, L. Thresh, D. Murphy, G. Kearney, "A Perceptual Evaluation of Individual and
Non-Individual HRTFs: A Case Study of the SADIE II Database", Applied Sciences 8(11), 2029, 2018.
